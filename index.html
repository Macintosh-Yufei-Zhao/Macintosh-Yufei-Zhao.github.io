Research Interests

  • Network Science and Biological Networks: I am curious about how biological networks function properly through specific topology, and how these topology evolved. I am determined to find a way to analyze the biological network with more direct or simple methods, and work on the non-linear properties of biological networks.
    • My undergraduate research focuses on the network behind the circadian clock.
  • Complex Systems, Chaos and Emergence: It is my belief that emergence is the future of life sciences. The fact that intelligence, self-organization and other complicated behaviours emerge when system size grows to a certain threshold is so fascinating and the mechanism behind each individual is so simple that I want to find more about it.
  • Deep Learning, LLM and Neuron Networks: Although I am not majoring in AI, the development of AI will definitely affect everything. I want to know how AI models make ‘decisions’, think ‘logically’ and ‘evolve’ with training. Also, this is without doubt an example of emergence.
